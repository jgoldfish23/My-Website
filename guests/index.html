Flying in

San Diego International (SAN) is about 20 minutes away. Rent a car or grab a ride-share — parking near the park is street parking, so give yourself a little extra time.

Staying over

We have a courtesy room block at Inn by the Sea, two blocks from the ocean — details and the booking link are in Where to stay below.

Making a trip of it

You're in La Jolla between Christmas and New Year's — the sea lions at the cove, Torrey Pines, and Balboa Park are all close.

Where to stay

Our room block

We have courtesy wedding rates at Inn by the Sea at La Jolla — in the heart of the Village, two blocks from the ocean, and a short walk from the Wedding Bowl. The rates run the week around December 28–31, 2026, and single-night stays are fine.

Wedding rates

$179 / night + tax — Standard room
$259 / night + tax — Ocean-view room (floors 3–5 — these go fast)

Every room: king or two queens, balcony or patio, fridge and microwave, free Wi-Fi, heated outdoor pool, no resort fee, $10/day parking. Check-in 4 PM · check-out noon.

How to book

Call 1-800-526-4545 and ask for the Golding and Jenkins Wedding rate — or book online in our room block (the link works until mid-November; after that, call).

No deposit, and you can cancel up to 24 hours before arrival. The hotel expects to sell out that weekend — book early.
